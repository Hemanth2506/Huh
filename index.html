04 / SPECIFICATION

Technical summary

DETECTION
Electrochemical Impedance Spectroscopy (EIS) — proposed concept for tracking particle concentration.
MATERIALS
  • Mesh
  • Activated carbon (×2 stages)
  • Coconut fiber
  • Cotton + AISI 5052 aluminum alloy
  • Vetiver root
  • Zeolite stone
TARGET
Approximately 80–85% microplastic removal, subject to experimental validation.
STATUS
Concept and simulation stage — not yet lab-tested or certified.
